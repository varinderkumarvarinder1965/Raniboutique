Our story

Meet Rani

For more than two decades, Rani has been turning simple lengths of fabric into outfits people love to wear. What began as a small sewing machine at home has grown into a trusted neighbourhood tailoring studio.

She believes a perfect fit is more than measurements — it is about listening, understanding how you want to feel, and adding that little extra care to every seam. From everyday Punjabi suits to bridal pieces, each garment leaves her table finished by hand.
